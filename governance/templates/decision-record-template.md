# Decision

## Context

Describe the decision context, problem, and why it matters.

## Decision

State the decision clearly.

## Evidence

Document the evidence, research, constraints, and product considerations that informed the decision.

## Alternatives considered

List the options that were considered and why they were rejected or deferred.

## Consequences

Describe the expected consequences, trade-offs, and follow-up work.

## Related UX knowledge

Link to the standards, patterns, or models affected by this decision.

## Date

Date of decision.

## Status

Indicate whether this is active, superseded, or archival.
