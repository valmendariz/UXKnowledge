# Decision Records

This directory preserves the rationale behind significant UX decisions.

## Purpose

Decision records are intended to explain why a major UX change happened, what alternatives were considered, and what evidence or constraints shaped the outcome.

## Rules

- Do not create a decision record for trivial edits or formatting changes.
- A decision record should answer why the decision was made, not just what the decision was.
- Keep the record tied to relevant standards, patterns, research, or glossary updates.
- Use the template in [../templates/decision-record-template.md](../templates/decision-record-template.md) unless there is a strong reason to adapt it.

## Typical cases

- changing an active UX standard
- adopting or retiring a reusable pattern
- clarifying terminology with organizational impact
- resolving a contradiction between guidance sources
- documenting a major design-system decision
