# Research

This folder holds research summaries, evidence syntheses, and findings that may inform UX standards and patterns. Research is evidence, not automatically action-ready guidance.

## Purpose

Use this area for:

- summarizing studies and observations
- collecting findings from interviews, usability tests, and field work
- identifying patterns and contradictions in observed behavior
- preserving source references and contextual caveats

## Rules

- Research findings must remain traceable to their sources.
- Raw evidence should not be mistaken for validated UX standards.
- When research conflicts with an established standard, the standard should remain the controlling artifact unless a documented governance decision changes it.
- If findings are not yet actionable, they may remain in research until a human review determines whether they support a standard or pattern.

## Typical content

- synthesis notes
- annotated summaries
- findings tied to evidence
- open questions and gaps
